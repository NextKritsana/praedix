Chain hopping
Chain hopping is the process of transferring assets or tokens from one
blockchain network to another. The term is primarily used in the
cryptocurrency space, especially in fast-moving market environments,
when investors want to take advantage of price differences or move
assets to another blockchain that offers better features, security, and fee
structures, to move assets to another blockchain that offers better
features, security, or fee structures.
Price arbitrage: To profit from price differences between different
exchanges or blockchain networks.
Get better features or services: Move to another blockchain that offers
faster transaction speeds, lower transaction fees, higher security, etc.
Regulatory evasion: Moving assets to another blockchain to escape
regulation in a particular country or region.
Moving assets to another blockchain.
Chain hopping can be executed using technologies such as cross-chain
bridges, atomic swaps, or decentralized finance (DeFi) platforms. These
technologies allow users to exchange tokens issued on one blockchain for
equivalent tokens on another blockchain, allowing them to move assets
efficiently without complicated intermediaries or high fees.
