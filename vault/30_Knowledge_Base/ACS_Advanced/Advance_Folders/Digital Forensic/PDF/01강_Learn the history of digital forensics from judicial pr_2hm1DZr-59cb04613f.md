Analyzing the Frye and Daubert Standards
Scientific evidence must be widely accepted by experts in the field before it can be used as evidence in court.
1. Whether the theory or technique for which the evidence is offered has been subjected to peer review and publication
2. Whether the theory or technique has been widely recognized
3. The error rate of the theory or technique
4. Whether the theory or technique passes the "generally accepted" test
5. The judge will determine whether the scientific evidence is relevant and directly related to the case at hand
